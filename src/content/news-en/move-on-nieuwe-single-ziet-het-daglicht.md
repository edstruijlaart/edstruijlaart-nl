I can't stress enough how excited I am to share my latest single "Move On" with you! This track means an enormous amount to me and I can't wait to hear what you think of it.

The new single is a journey through my thoughts and feelings, cast in that familiar 'Ed Sound' you know and love. This song is about resilience, about getting up and moving forward regardless of the obstacles that come our way. The lyrics take you inward and the melodies will grab hold of you.

A real goosebump moment for me was bringing in Aaron Sterling, the incredible drummer you know from artists like John Mayer, Taylor Swift and Harry Styles. His rhythmic magic has taken "Move On" to another level, and I couldn't be prouder of the result.

You can listen to the new single right now on all your favourite streaming platforms, including Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube. I genuinely hope this song moves you the way it moved me when I was making it.

Want to stay up to date with my musical adventures? Keep an eye on my website and social media. I can't wait to hear what you think of "Move On". Thank you for your unconditional support, and let's enjoy this new chapter in my musical world together!

Rock on, Ed Struijlaart
