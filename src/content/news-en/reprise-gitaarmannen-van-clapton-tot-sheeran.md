Great news! In 2019 I'm going back into theaters with my Gitaarmannen: van Clapton tot Sheeran (Guitar Heroes: From Clapton to Sheeran) theater tour. Last year was a huge success and nearly every show was sold out! Plenty of reason for the theater directors to book the reprise tour. Last year we did 26, this time there are 44 shows on the schedule! I'd love for you to be there!
