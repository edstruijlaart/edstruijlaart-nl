Our neighbours to the south are doing good things. They've discovered What I Like About You. And not just any station - the BIGGEST one in Flanders (Radio 2 VRT) has added my song to their playlist. What many people don't know is that I have very distant Belgian roots. Struijlaart is a name that's common in the border region of Zeeuws-Vlaanderen (Dutch Flanders) and Belgium, and my grandparents came from there too. So that gives it a nice extra shine :). I'll have to head down that way soon and get to know my new Belgian friends!
