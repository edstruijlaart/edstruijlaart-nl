This Sunday too (29 March) there will be a Quarantine Sessions episode. No theme this time, just music I feel like playing. You can of course share your input via the chat, or by sending a WhatsApp or voice note to 0682112502. The stream I'll be broadcasting on next Sunday is below. A virtual ticket can be purchased via http://bit.ly/quarantainesessions

What a mess all of this is. I genuinely hope you're all safe and doing well with your loved ones! Because of the Corona crisis, everything has gone sideways. All my shows have been cancelled up to at least the end of this month. That's obviously a major source of income for me, and a few weeks ago I asked on Twitter, out loud, how I was supposed to manage this as a self-employed person and breadwinner. Marijke Roskam replied and suggested setting up a livestream where people could make a voluntary contribution. A kind of busking, but in quarantine. The idea for the Quarantine Sessions was born.

Every Sunday evening I'll go live at 8:30 pm on Facebook, YouTube and Twitter. You can buy a virtual ticket via bit.ly/quarantainesessions.
