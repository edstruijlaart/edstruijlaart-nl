Hey, my new single came out this week and it's called 'House on Fire'. The track is taken from the album 'Closer Than Skin' which was released in October 2014.

I recorded my second album 'Closer Than Skin' in London.

"In London I found the peace and space to take stock of everything I'd experienced over the past few years, to see where I stood, and to pour it all into these 11 songs. Together with Carey Willetts I really got inside the songs to entrust them to the album as honestly and sincerely as possible."
