Right now I'm in theaters for the first time with Gitaarmannen: van Clapton tot Sheeran (Guitar Heroes: From Clapton to Sheeran)! A tribute to my first great love: the guitar! Together with my band I pay tribute to the instrument that changed my life forever. Through songs by my guitar heroes, and of course my own songs too. Expect songs by Clapton, Mayer, Santana, Hendrix, Ed Sheeran!
