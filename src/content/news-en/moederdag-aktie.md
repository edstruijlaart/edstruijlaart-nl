**MOTHER'S DAY**

It's almost here, and maybe you're still looking for a Corona-proof original gift? Look no further, because I'm going to help you surprise your mum!

There are two ways to do it:

**1. Online Living Room Concert.**

A living room concert via a private stream that you both log into at the agreed time. Beforehand you record a video or audio message for your mum that will play at the start of the stream. This is followed by an exclusive performance by Ed Struijlaart, lasting 15 minutes. After the show you'll receive the concert as a download.

**2. A Mother's Day Serenade**

Surprise your mum with a personalised message recorded by Ed, followed by the mother of all Mother's Day classics: Heintje - Mama (in a blazing 2020 version). You'll receive this serenade as an mp3 in your inbox.
