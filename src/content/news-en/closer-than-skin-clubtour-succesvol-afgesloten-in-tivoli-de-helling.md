Last Saturday the Closer Than Skin club tour came to a blazing and screaming end at Tivoli de Helling in Utrecht. 6 shows in 3 weeks with an incredible band, incredible audiences and great venues. A happy Struijlaart is typing this and can only say: hopefully there will be a sequel, because this leaves me wanting more. Very soon EDTV #3, with footage from the last 2 shows of the Closer Than Skin Club Tour. For now: the photos and some clips. From here a big thank you to my band: Leon Schmitz (drums), Michel van der Zanden (guitar/keys) and Lars van Starrenburg (bass guitar). And the tech guys: Arne van der Heijden (monitors) and Bart Schatteleijn (FOH).

I already wrote it above, but I'll say it again: it was an incredible experience to be on the road with this crew. The coming months have a number of festivals planned, including the Southpark festival, Onderstroom Festival and the Schollenpop festival in Scheveningen.
