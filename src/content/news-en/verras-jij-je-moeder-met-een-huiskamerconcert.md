10 May is Mother's Day again. Yeah, mums rule! Of course, every year you think: what the *bleep* am I going to give this time to show mum how much I appreciate her? Another Rituals gift set? Or that cloyingly sweet High Tea again? No way, this year you're going to show up with something original: a Mother's Day living room concert!

On Mother's Day I want to put a number of mums in the spotlight with a short living room concert. At a ridiculously low special price. Interested? Just drop me an email: moederdag@edstruijlaart.nl. I'll let you know the details!

The Mother's Day living room concert promotion was a great success! In total I got to make 5 mums happy in Deventer, Amersfoort, Sint Pancras, Den Haag and Alphen a/d Rijn. There was also a very special moment in Deventer. The man of the house proposed to his girlfriend! And on her very first Mother's Day as a brand new mum. How beautiful is that? I headed home with a warm feeling. It's so great to be able to create these kinds of moments with music! :D. The question immediately came in whether I was also going to do this for Father's Day. After long deliberation I decided to keep it to this Mother's Day promotion for now. Who knows, maybe I'll do it again next year and put dads in the proverbial spotlight!
