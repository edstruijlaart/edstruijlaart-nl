The past year didn't bring us much. Or did it? Secretly I've gained a lot of valuable new insights. Which ones? I'm saving those for the stream. Of course I'll play some music for you, and you can join the conversation via Zoom (FUN!). You can also react from behind your keyboard through the YouTube chat. We'll keep it nice and light.
