From 21 September I'm touring the country with my first theater show 'Gitaarmannen: van Clapton tot Sheeran' (Guitar Heroes: From Clapton to Sheeran), and I thought that was a perfect moment to write a song about the instrument that has given me so much. That song became 'Guitar'. Last 16 August I turned 39 and I realised that exactly 30 years ago, on my 9th birthday, I got my very first guitar. It was a super cheap model costing 75 guilders. You can see it on the artwork of the single. That photo was taken on that same 9th birthday.

I still remember that day clearly. In the morning I'd had my first guitar lesson, and when I got home I went straight to practising furiously. That didn't go without a struggle, and I clearly remember the furious practising turning into furious foot-stomping. 'It. Won't. Work!!11'. It was a tough start. But I quickly felt that we were going to be inseparable. The guitar has brought me so many beautiful things. It was a companion during the moments I felt alone. It brought comfort when loved ones passed away. It brought happiness when I decided to pursue my musical dream, and took me to the United States, England and Italy, among other places. It provided the soundtrack of my life.

As of today, Guitar is available to stream and download everywhere. Feel free to request it from your favourite radio DJ. Radio 2 DJ Wouter van der Goes has already named it as his DJ Favourite for the coming week. A good start! :D
