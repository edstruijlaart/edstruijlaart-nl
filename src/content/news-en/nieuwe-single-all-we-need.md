On Friday 5 April, singer-songwriter Ed Struijlaart released his new single 'All We Need'. The song is a preview of a busy year ahead for Struijlaart, one that's building towards his 'Gitaarmannen 3: John Mayer' (Guitar Heroes 3: John Mayer) theater tour.

What is All We Need about?

Ed explains: "'All We Need' came about on the day of the Giro555 fundraising campaign for Turkey. My 7-year-old daughter spontaneously offered her savings to help out. Her gesture inspired the writing session with producer Lukaz and singer-songwriter Philine that was planned for that day.

Maybe we as humans occasionally need that childlike openness to solve our problems and overcome our differences."

A nice detail: Ed's children both sing along on All We Need, and his partner made the artwork.
