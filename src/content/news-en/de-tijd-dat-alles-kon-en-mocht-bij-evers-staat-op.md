What an incredible honour: in the final week that Edwin Evers can be heard on Radio 538 with his legendary morning show 'Evers Staat Op' (Evers Gets Up), I get to come in and play my new single!

I wrote De Tijd Dat Alles Kon En Mocht (The Time When Everything Was Possible) together with Peter Slager (BLØF) for the musical Soof. Together with Peter I wrote all the music, including the heartfelt 'Deze Armen' (These Arms). Which we wrote together with the brilliant Claudia de Breij.
