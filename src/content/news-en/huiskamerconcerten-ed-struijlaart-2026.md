I'm doing living room concerts again.

I've been doing them since 2012. And every single time I'm amazed at how special these evenings are.

One voice. One guitar. A stack of songs and good stories.

Think of it as a mini theater show, but at your home. No stage. No distance. When the weather's nice I'm just as happy playing in the garden or on the patio.

A living room concert is not background music. It's listening, being together, switching everything off for a while. We always make something special out of it.

Sometimes I play songs from my Gitaarmannen (Guitar Heroes) theater shows, sometimes my own work. Often a combination. It depends on the evening and the people in the room.

I play completely acoustic in principle, but I can bring a PA system if needed. All you need to provide is a space and an audience.

Perfect for birthdays, anniversaries, farewells, or simply because you want to celebrate something. Groups of 10 to 50 people. Intimate, personal and close.

Curious? Book directly via [boeken.edstruijlaart.nl](https://boeken.edstruijlaart.nl) or get in touch at ed@earswantmusic.nl.
