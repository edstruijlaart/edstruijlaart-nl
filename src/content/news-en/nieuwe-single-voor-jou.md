VOOR JOU (FOR YOU)!
Simply a lovely song. That's what Paskal Jakobsen from BLØF and I found exciting to make. A lovely song about a love that's just beginning to bloom. A lovely song about how love develops over the years. Hear in it whatever you want to hear. The fact is that it's available to listen to on all platforms from today, and that the world could really use some love right now with all the polarisation around us.

Next week a beautiful lyric video will follow. So keep an eye out. Sharing is appreciated, requesting it at your favourite station too. Go for it and spread the love :D
