A living room concert with Ed Struijlaart is a unique way to enjoy his music. Instead of sitting in a large concert hall, you get to experience this talented singer-songwriter perform in the intimate setting of someone's home. Ed Struijlaart is known for his beautiful voice and excellent guitar playing. In this article we share five tips for organising a living room concert with Ed Struijlaart and enjoying an unforgettable experience.

## 5 tips for organising a successful living room concert

1. **Choose the right location.** The location of the living room concert is crucial to the experience. Make sure the space is large enough for the artist and the audience, but not so large that the intimate atmosphere gets lost.

2. **Invite the right people.** The guests you invite largely determine the atmosphere. Choose people who are genuinely interested in the music and open to an intimate and personal setting. Don't forget to send invitations in good time and limit the number of guests to what the space allows.

3. **Discuss the setlist with Ed Struijlaart.** One of the biggest advantages of a living room concert with Ed Struijlaart is that he tailors the setlist to the wishes of the host. Discuss which songs you'd like to hear before the concert and let Ed know what you and your guests love. That way the concert becomes a truly bespoke experience.

4. **Create the right atmosphere.** The atmosphere of the living room concert is hugely important for the overall experience. Get the lighting and decoration right to transform the space into an intimate and atmospheric venue. And don't forget to arrange some snacks and drinks for the artist and guests.

5. **Enjoy the [experience](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1iqI74DRdwQ).** When everything is well organised, you can sit back and enjoy Ed Struijlaart's music without a care. Remember to turn your phone off and focus entirely on the music and the unique experience. Let yourself be carried away by Ed Struijlaart's beautiful voice and musical talents, and create memories you won't forget anytime soon.

## Conclusion

A living room concert with Ed Struijlaart is a unique way to enjoy his music. The intimate setting and personal approach of the artist make it an unforgettable experience. Find a suitable location, invite the right people, discuss the setlist with Ed Struijlaart, and above all make sure you enjoy this unique experience yourself!
