Hope you're doing well! I hope you've had or are having a nice holiday. I've also unplugged for a bit and recharged myself, lovely! Now that things are getting back up to speed I want to draw your attention to the following:

On December 23rd I'm doing my only full band show of this year and I'd like to warmly invite you. It's just before Christmas, so I'm turning it into a special X-Mas Pre-Party. Expect some big Christmas hits given a personal twist. And of course plenty of room for the familiar Ed songs.

I talk about it a bit here:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dKiDdp2CF4w

I've unfortunately seen up close what a terrible disease MS is. My cousin passed away two years ago from the effects of this illness and it affected me deeply. Other people close to me are also dealing with it, and sadly there's still no cure. So any money I can raise through my music to support the research is very welcome in my opinion. There will be donation boxes during the evening, I'm donating my fee and Paard van Troje (the venue) is donating the service fees. Hopefully we can make a difference together.

I'm busy arranging some great guest musicians for the evening. How cool would it be to have some extra flair. Unfortunately many of my musical friends are on holiday during this period or are completely booked up and can't make it. Do you have a suggestion for a fun guest artist I should approach? Send me an email or leave a message in the comments.

If you're planning to come to my show on the 23rd: let me know in the comments, so I can account for the numbers.
