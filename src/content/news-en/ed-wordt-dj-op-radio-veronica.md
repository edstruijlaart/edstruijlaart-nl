NEWSFLASH! I'm becoming a DJ at Radio Veronica!

Under the leadership of station manager Rob Stenders, Radio Veronica transforms on June 28th into a haven for music lovers. So incredibly proud to have been asked for this! Radio Veronica is the sound of my youth, and the idea that I get to be part of it myself is something I never dared to dream.

I'll have a regular slot on Saturday mornings from 9:00 to 12:00. Expect a great set of music you'll truly love, and I'm also picking up my podcast Gitaarmannen (Guitar Heroes) again. It'll play an important role in the programme. I'll soon be announcing the first guest of season 3!

For the rest of the refreshed schedule I'll point you to [www.radioveronica.nl](https://www.radioveronica.nl)

HOW AMAZING!! AAAAAAAHHHHHH!

ps. don't worry, I'm still making music!
