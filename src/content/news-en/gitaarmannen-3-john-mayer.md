After a sold-out first run, Gitaarmannen 3: John Mayer (Guitar Heroes 3: John Mayer) is returning to the theatres!

Ed Struijlaart's show is a musical tribute to the guitarist and songwriter of the past twenty years: John Mayer.

During the show Ed performs the most beautiful songs from Mayer's career together with his band - from Gravity to Slow Dancing in a Burning Room - and takes the audience into the remarkable story behind the music. Expect an evening of virtuoso guitar playing, recognisable hits and personal stories.

"John Mayer is the artist who made the guitar sexy again for me. With this show I pay tribute to his music and to the generation of guitarists he inspired." - Ed Struijlaart

Since Ed's appearance on Goedemorgen Nederland (Good Morning Netherlands, Dutch morning TV show) this morning, ticket sales have been going through the roof.

Want to be there? Don't wait too long - many venues are heading towards sold out!
