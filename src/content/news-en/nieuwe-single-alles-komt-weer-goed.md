Let me start by saying that this is a song I'd rather not have written. Every morning our daughter crawls into bed with us and asks "What are we doing today?" "Can I go to grandma's?" "Why aren't I going to school?" We try our best to explain what's going on. It's tough. For her and her little brother (he's eighteen months old, so thankfully doesn't quite understand yet). It's tough for us. It's tough for our parents. But above all you want to reassure your child, take their worries away. Everything will be alright. I keep repeating it, and eventually I'm sure it will be. Right?
