My Gitaarmannen (Guitar Heroes) tour is done and dusted. In total we played this show 70 times. It was one big party, but now it's time for new music: Next to Me! That's the name of my new single. It's a song that will hopefully make you long for spring, because I'm well and truly ready for it myself ;). What I find particularly special is that on drums I have none other than Aaron Sterling. Aaron is the drummer for John Mayer, but also Taylor Swift, Harry Styles and many other heavyweights in the contemporary pop world. It's an honour that he was willing to drum on my single! On top of that, I have my good friend Peter Slager from BLØF on bass guitar. With a rhythm section like that, what could go wrong, right? ;)

The song is already getting great pick-up and is already on the Radio 1 and Radio 2 VRT (Belgium) playlists. Listen to Next to Me via the following link and add it to your playlist: http://bit.ly/37GRFIw
