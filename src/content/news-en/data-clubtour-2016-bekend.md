After the great success of my club tour in 2015, I'm doing another run through the clubs this year. In total we'll hit 4 venues - and not the smallest ones. See all the dates and direct ticket links below. I hope to see you at one (or more) of them!

CLUB TOUR 2016
Oct 15 - Paradiso (upstairs hall), Amsterdam (tickets)
Oct 20 - Merleyn, Nijmegen (tickets)
Oct 21 - Paard, The Hague (tickets)
Oct 22 - Tivoli De Helling, Utrecht (tickets)

Ed Struijlaart club tour

What can you expect at one of Ed Struijlaart's club tour evenings? It's a bit different from a living room concert you might be used to from me. In the living room I tell more stories; during the tour I'll let the music do more of the talking. What exactly my band and I will bring you this year I can't tell you yet (we're still working that out). But at the very least I'll be presenting my new (still untitled) EP during the tour. Last year we made it a great all-round celebration. A nice feel-good evening where I alternate my own songs with a set of old blues numbers (my big inspiration). We also suddenly popped up completely acoustic in the middle of the room, out of nowhere. Just a taste of what an evening looks like. Oh, and coming soon there'll be more news about my upcoming new single. This is a special song, and I can already reveal it's something I've never done before... namely... a duet! With who exactly? I'd love to tell you... but I'm not revealing that just yet. It will be announced in due course.
