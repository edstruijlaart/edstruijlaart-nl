Ed Struijlaart & Lisa Lois - Made of Stars

After the success of Tricks Up My Sleeve it was time to release something new. It became Made of Stars, which is also my very first duet ever. I managed to get one of the best singers in the Netherlands to sing the song with me: Lisa Lois! That's right, you may know Lisa as the winner of X-Factor. She won the 2009 edition of the talent show with a widely acclaimed version of Hallelujah. I'm genuinely thrilled that she wanted to record this song with me. I wrote the song together with my good friend Niels Geusebroek, by the way.

A few weeks ago we visited Arno Krabman's Graveland Studio with the band, and in a single day we put the track down on tape. And since we were there anyway, we shot a music video at the same time. You can find it here:

Of course the song is also available on all digital platforms, and we'd absolutely love it if you'd share it. Because this song feels like a perfect match for the great weather!
