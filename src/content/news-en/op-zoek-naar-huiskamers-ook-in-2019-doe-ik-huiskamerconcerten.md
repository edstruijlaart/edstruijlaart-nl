So, the Gitaarmannen (Guitar Heroes) theater tour is wrapped up! Were you there? It was a resounding success, that's for sure. Who knows what next year has in store ;). Until then, I'm genuinely excited about the new year. In the first part of 2019 I want to focus on writing new material and recording a new EP. Something I've been doing for the past few years, and will happily keep doing, is testing new material in the living room! A living room concert, right at your home. It's really simple: you invite a bunch of friends and I come and play my songs and test new tracks on you. If you have something to celebrate, or just think "sounds great!", don't hesitate and send me an email to receive the terms and available dates. Email: huiskamer@edstruijlaart.nl
