It's coming out very soon, my new EP 'Alles Op Rood' (All In Red). That obviously calls for a little celebration. Normally you'd throw a release party, but that's a bit tricky with the coronavirus going around. So I came up with something different: a Livestream Listening Session! Right from your armchair, I'll take you through the songs on the EP. I'll play them for you and tell you the stories behind them. Up close and personal! You can ask questions and share your thoughts via the chat. Let's make it a fun evening. A bit of cheese, a beer or glass of wine. The works.

When?
Sunday 20/09

Where?
Via this link: https://studio.youtube.com/video/-e9hl-Af80E/livestreaming

After the session you'll be the first to be able to order the EP, WITH A DISCOUNT! I'll explain how that works during the livestream :).
