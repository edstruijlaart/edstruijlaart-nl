Last Friday the remix of Make It On Your Own was released. The remix was made by the young, promising producer duo Giraff (featuring among others the keyboard player from Rondé). The remix is signed to the world's biggest record label: Universal Music. I'm pretty proud of that :). The remix is already being picked up well by Spotify and has been added to various playlists. Curious to see how far this one goes :).
